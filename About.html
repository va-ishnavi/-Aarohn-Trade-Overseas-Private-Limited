Who We Are

Aarohn Trade Overseas Private Limited is a New Delhi based export company specializing in premium quality Pulses (Lentils) and Natural Soapnuts. We are committed to delivering products that meet international quality standards while maintaining reliability, transparency, and customer satisfaction.

Through strong sourcing networks and quality-focused operations, we serve importers, wholesalers, distributors, and food businesses across Europe, USA, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand.
